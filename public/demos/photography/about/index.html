The studio

A small team, in one city, since 2019

Cahaya Studio started with one camera and a rented room above a coffee shop on Jalan Gaya. The first year was mostly graduation portraits. The weddings came later, and the product work came because a client asked if we could shoot their menu.

We stayed small on purpose. Three people, one edit style, and enough time between jobs to actually finish the last one properly. Everything is shot and edited in house, nothing is sent out to an overseas retoucher.

Most of our work is in Kota Kinabalu, but Sabah is big and we like the drive. We have shot on Kudat beaches at 5am, in Sandakan boardrooms, and on a boat off Semporna with all the gear in dry bags.
