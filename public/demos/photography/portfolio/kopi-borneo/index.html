Kopi Borneo

Forty six catalogue images for a coffee roaster moving from a market stall to online orders.

The brief was simple. Every bag had to look the same from every angle, and the images had to work on a phone at thumbnail size.

We built one lighting setup, locked the camera down, and shot the whole range in a single day. Consistency came from the setup, not from retouching.

The lifestyle set was shot at their roastery the following morning while they were actually working.
