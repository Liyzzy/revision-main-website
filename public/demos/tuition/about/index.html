About Us

Started by two tutors who kept running out of whiteboard space

Brightpath started as weekend tuition for a handful of neighbourhood kids. As more families asked for a spot, weekend sessions turned into a proper timetable, a rented space, and eventually a small team of subject instructors who all shared the same idea, that classes work best small, structured, and easy for parents to keep track of.

Today Brightpath runs classes across mathematics, science, languages, coding, and music, still in small groups, still with a real instructor who knows every student's name.

What we care about

Progress over perfection

We track effort and improvement, not just grades on a page.

Every student learns differently

Instructors adjust pace and explanation style class to class, not just student to student.

Parents stay in the loop

Schedules, instructors, and enrollments are visible any time through your member login.

Our space

  • Air conditioned classrooms with a low instructor to student ratio
  • A dedicated computer lab for coding classes
  • A quiet music room with an acoustic piano
  • A small reading corner for time between classes
