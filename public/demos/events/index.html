What is inside every invite

The parts that usually get handled in a group chat, built into the page instead.

Digital e-invites

One link that opens on any phone.

Every event gets its own page with a cover, a countdown, the full programme and a share sheet that drops a ready written message into WhatsApp. No app to install, nothing to print, and you can fix a typo after sending it out.

Multi event RSVP

One guest, several sessions, separate headcounts.

A wedding usually is not one event. Guests tick the sessions they are coming to, put a number of pax against each, and the host sees a clean count per session instead of a group chat full of maybes.

Maps integration

Directions that actually open the right app.

Each venue shows an embedded map plus one tap buttons for Waze and Google Maps. Coordinates are stored per session, so the nikah and the reception can sit in two different towns without confusing anyone.

Wishes wall

Guests leave a note, everyone reads it.

A public wall of messages, posted straight from the invite. Turn on moderation and nothing appears until the host approves it. Export the whole thing afterwards as a keepsake.

Gifts and payments

Money gift, or a real ticket price.

For a free event, show bank details and a DuitNow QR for guests who want to send something. For a paid event, list ticket tiers, take the buyer details and the transfer receipt, and track who has paid from the dashboard.

Host dashboard

Numbers in one place.

Total pax per session, the guest list, pending wishes, and contributions received. Protected by a login, so only the host and whoever they add can see it.
