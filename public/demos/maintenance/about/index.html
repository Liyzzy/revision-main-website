About us

A small team that actually turns up

FixPoint Maintenance Services Sdn Bhd started as one technician with a van and a phone. Twelve years later we are a handful of licensed tradesmen covering the west coast, and the way we work has not changed much.

Most maintenance companies grow by hiring fast and hoping the work holds up. We went the other way. Every technician here has served their time in the trade, and the person who quotes your job is usually the person who turns up to do it.

That is also why we do not chase every job. If a repair is not worth the money, we will tell you to replace the unit instead. If it is not our trade, we will point you at someone we would use ourselves. Losing one callout is cheaper than losing a customer.

Today we look after households on maintenance plans, shops and restaurants on service contracts, and a steady stream of one off emergencies. Same standard for all of them.
