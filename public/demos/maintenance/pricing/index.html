Questions

About our pricing

Do you charge for a quote?

Quotes over the phone or WhatsApp are free. If we have to come out and inspect before we can price the job, the standard callout applies, and it is absorbed into the invoice if you go ahead with the work.

Why is the price a starting rate rather than a fixed one?

Two aircon services are rarely the same job. The starting rate covers a standard unit in normal condition. Anything beyond that, a seized fan or a leak inside the wall for example, is quoted before we touch it.

What is included in a maintenance plan?

Scheduled servicing, priority response and discounted labour on anything that breaks between visits. Parts and materials are charged separately at cost plus handling.

How do I pay?

Bank transfer, DuitNow QR or cash on completion. Business contracts are invoiced monthly with 14 day terms.
